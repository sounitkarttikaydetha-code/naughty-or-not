# FATF Access Inquiry & Machine-Readable Feed Request

> **Note:** Draft request prepared for review. Do not send automatically. To be submitted manually by the compliance officer via the FATF Contact Page (`https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/contact-us.html`).

---

**Subject:** Inquiry regarding automated/machine-readable feed access for FATF Public Statements (High-Risk and Other Monitored Jurisdictions)

**To:** Financial Action Task Force (FATF Secretariat)  
**From:** Compliance & Regulatory Engineering Team, Clearance Check  

**Inquiry Text:**

Dear FATF Secretariat,

We maintain an offline-first compliance and sanctions screening platform used by regulated financial and compliance analysts to assess jurisdictional risk in accordance with FATF standards and recommendations.

Currently, we ingest and cross-reference the public statements for:
1. High-Risk Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action (Black list)
2. Jurisdictions under Increased Monitoring (Grey list)

We respect the access controls and terms of service on `www.fatf-gafi.org`. To ensure our local regulatory copies remain synchronized following each triannual plenary (February, June, and October) without generating unnecessary web traffic, we would like to inquire:

1. Does the FATF offer an official machine-readable feed (such as JSON, XML, or RSS/Atom) for public plenary statements and jurisdiction lists?
2. Alternatively, does the FATF permit low-volume, scheduled automated retrieval (e.g. once post-plenary, using a declared User-Agent with contact details and conditional HTTP headers `If-Modified-Since`)?
3. If an API or structured distribution service exists for compliance vendors and researchers, could you kindly provide registration details or applicable licensing guidelines?

In the interim, we rely on official national government republications (such as HM Treasury advisory notices, FinCEN advisories, and IFSCA public statements) alongside analyst-assisted imports. We would welcome the opportunity to connect directly to an authoritative FATF machine-readable service if available.

Thank you very much for your time and guidance.

Yours sincerely,  
Compliance & Regulatory Engineering Team  
Clearance Check (`https://github.com/skarttik/Clearance-Check`)
